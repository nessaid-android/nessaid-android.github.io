The rules of Teen Patti

Three cards each, and everybody's boot in the pot.

On your turn, bet or raise. A blind player, who has not looked, bets the stake; a seen player bets twice it. Looking is free, once, and for the hand. Pack to fold: what you staked stays in the pot.

When two are left, either may ask for a show, at the stake if blind or twice it if seen, and the better hand takes the pot. Best first: trail (three of a kind), pure sequence, sequence, colour, pair, high card; A-2-3 is the best run.

The table can set a blind limit that makes you look, and a pot limit that shows everybody down.
