Express card games

Cards for the journey. No internet needed.

Rummy, Teen Patti, Twenty Eight, Call Break, Poker and Bridge for friends on a train, a bus or in the same room, each on their own phone. The phones connect to each other directly, so the game keeps going with no signal, no mobile data and no sign-in.

One table, six games

All six are built on the same card table, so what you learn in one is true in the others: the host arranges the seats and proposes the start, everybody with a phone has to agree, bots take any seat nobody is sitting in, and a phone that drops out of range can come back to the same table. Your cards are only ever on your own phone, and there is no real money anywhere.
