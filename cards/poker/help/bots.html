Bots

A bot is a player with no phone: the host's phone plays for it. Seat one with Add bots when a table is short.

In Settings you can rename your bots and set how much each one thinks: its memory dial, from 0 to 100, is the chance each turn that it studies what everybody has shown, rather than playing its own hand alone.
