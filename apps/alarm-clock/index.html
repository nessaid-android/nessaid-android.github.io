Alarm Clock Ultimate

Alarm Clock Ultimate is an alarm clock built around one promise: the alarm rings when it should, the way you set it up, and never fails silently.

About Alarm Clock Ultimate

Every alarm has a real repeat schedule rather than a row of weekday boxes: every day, chosen weekdays, a day of the month, a weekday of the month such as the second Tuesday, or once a year, with exceptions and a skip for just the next one. A vacation setting holds every alarm until you are back, without turning any of them off.

Each alarm and each timer carries its own alert: tone, volume and gradual build-up, vibration pattern, a flashing torch, a spoken label, how long it rings, and how snooze works. To make sure you are awake, an alarm can ask for a sum, a shake of the phone, or a held tap before it stops. None of them can lock you out: they keep the alarm ringing while you try, and fall back to a plain tap if the phone or the moment cannot manage them.

Timers and stopwatches sit alongside the alarms, and a calendar tab shows what the day holds. A missed alarm is always reported, and an alarm history shows exactly when each alarm rang and how it was stopped.

Features

  • Repeat schedules: daily, weekdays, monthly by date or weekday, yearly, with exceptions
  • Skip the next alarm, and a vacation mode for all of them
  • Per-alarm tone, volume, build-up, vibration, torch and speech
  • Dismissal challenges: maths, shake, or tap and hold
  • Flexible snooze
  • Multiple timers and stopwatches with laps
  • Read-only calendar agenda
  • Home screen widget and a Quick Settings timer tile
  • Works with "set an alarm" requests from voice assistants and other apps
  • Alarm history, and notifications for missed alarms
  • Export and import of alarms, timers and settings
  • Optional password lock for alarm changes
  • 12 colour themes and 26 languages
Alarm Clock Ultimate is in development and is not on Google Play yet.
