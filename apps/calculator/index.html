Nessaid Calculator

Nessaid Calculator is a calculator styled like a hardware device, with a seven-segment LCD display, and a set of converters and finance tools behind it.

About Nessaid Calculator

Switch between four calculators: basic, scientific, programmer and statistics. Scientific mode works in degrees, radians or grads and shows numbers in normal, fixed, scientific or engineering form. Programmer mode works in hexadecimal, decimal, octal and binary with a word size you choose. The display holds 10, 15, 20 or 40 digits, and earlier expressions are a key press away.

Beyond the keypad, a unit converter covers fourteen kinds of measurement, twelve finance calculators handle everyday money questions from discounts to loan EMIs, and a currency converter uses current exchange rates.

Features

  • Basic, scientific, programmer and statistics modes
  • Seven-segment LCD display with 10, 15, 20 or 40 digits
  • Expression history
  • Unit converter: length, mass, temperature, area, volume, time, speed, angle, data, force, pressure, energy, power and torque
  • Finance: percentages, discount, margin and markup, simple and compound interest, EMI, SIP, lumpsum, CAGR and GST
  • Currency converter with rates updated from the internet
  • Dark and light themes
  • A built-in operations guide
  • 13 languages
Nessaid Calculator is in development and is not on Google Play yet.
