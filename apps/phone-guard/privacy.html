Privacy policy: Phone Guard - privacy policy

Last updated 28 November 2026

Phone Guard is made by Nessaid Android Studio (nessaid.android@gmail.com). This page says what the app reads, what it keeps, and what leaves your phone.

What stays on your phone

Your guards, your places and their Wi-Fi networks, the Bluetooth devices you chose, your data limits, your settings and the PIN are kept on your phone only. The PIN is stored as a salted hash, never as the digits. None of it is sent to us or to anyone else. Your guards and settings may be included in Android's own backup of your phone, if you have it on; the PIN is not.

What the app reads, and why

  • Wi-Fi network names and access points - for the Leave and Reach guards, to know when you are at a place you marked. Android allows an app to read these only with location permission, and while the app is closed only with "Allow all the time". Phone Guard never reads your location (no GPS, no location requests); the network name and its access point are the only things it reads, and they stay on your phone. You can use every other guard without location permission.
  • Bluetooth connections (the "Nearby devices" permission) - for the Bluetooth guard, to know when a device you chose disconnects.
  • Mobile data counts (usage access, granted in Settings) - for data limits, the amount of mobile data used by the phone or one app. Read on the phone only.
  • The motion and proximity sensors, and the charger - for Move, Pocket and Unplug, while those guards are on.
  • The list of apps on your launcher - only when you choose an app for a data limit, to show it.

Ads

Phone Guard is free and shows ads, from Google AdMob and the Yandex Advertising Network. Those networks may collect your device's advertising ID and information about the ads shown and tapped, and Yandex's SDK includes AppMetrica, which sends Yandex crash and performance reports. Their own policies apply: Google (https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads) and Yandex (https://yandex.com/legal/confidential/). Where the law requires it, you are asked for consent first, and you can change your answer in the app's settings. You can reset your advertising ID in Android's settings at any time.

Remove ads

One optional purchase removes the ads. It is made through Google Play; we receive no payment details. The app keeps a record on the phone that the purchase was made.

Children

Phone Guard is not directed at children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the date above changes with it. Questions: nessaid.android@gmail.com.
