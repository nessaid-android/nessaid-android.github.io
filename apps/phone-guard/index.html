Phone Guard

Phone Guard rings when something about your phone changes - and the alarms that guard against a thief stop only with your PIN.

The guards

  • Unplug. Charging in a café, a library or an airport: if someone pulls the charger, the phone rings until your PIN is entered.
  • Move. Rings when the phone is lifted or moved, after a few seconds to put it down.
  • Pocket. Rings when the phone is taken out of your pocket or bag.
  • Bluetooth left behind. Rings when earbuds, a watch or the car disconnect, with your own message spoken aloud.
  • Leave or reach a place. Home, Office or a place of your own, known by its Wi-Fi networks: a reminder as you leave or arrive.
  • Data limit. Rings when mobile data passes a limit you set, for the day or the month, for the phone or one app.

What a thief cannot do

  • Stop an anti-theft alarm without your PIN
  • Tap Stop on the lock screen: those alarms have no buttons there
  • Press Back to get rid of the alarm screen

Private by design

Nothing about your guards, places, devices or data use leaves your phone. Phone Guard never reads where you are: location permission is used only to read the name of the Wi-Fi network, as Android requires.
