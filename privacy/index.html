Website privacy

Last updated 17 September 2026

This page covers this website only. Each app and game has its own privacy policy, linked from its page.

What this website collects: nothing

There are no accounts, no forms, no analytics and no advertising on this website. It is a set of static pages served by GitHub Pages.

What is stored in your browser

If you choose Mobile or Desktop layout, that choice is saved in your browser's local storage so the next page uses it too. It never leaves your browser. Choosing Auto removes it.

Hosting

The website is hosted by GitHub, which receives the technical information any web server receives, such as your IP address, to deliver the pages. See GitHub's privacy statement.

Videos

How-to videos come from YouTube, but nothing is loaded from YouTube until you press play on a video. The player is then loaded from YouTube's privacy-enhanced domain, and YouTube's own privacy policy applies to it.

Links to Google Play

Links to Google Play take you to Google's site, where Google's privacy policy applies.
