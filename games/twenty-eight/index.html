Twenty Eight

Twenty Eight is the trick-taking card game played across Kerala and India, for four players in two pairs or six in two teams of three, each on their own phone.

About Twenty Eight

The pack is the thirty-two cards from seven to ace, with the sixes added for six players. Jacks are highest and worth three points, nines two, aces and tens one each: twenty-eight points in all. After half the cards are dealt, players bid for how many points their side will take, and the highest bidder sets a trump by placing a card face down.

Play follows the table's own habits. A player who cannot follow suit can ask for the hidden trump to be shown, a second round of bidding can raise the stakes, and a side can bid to take every trick. Game points carry over from hand to hand.

The host fills every seat, with bots for any seat without a player, and chooses the house rules: four or six players, direction of play, the opening bid, the second round, and whether asking for the trump is required.

Features

  • Four players in two pairs, or six in two teams
  • Bidding from 14, with an optional second round from 24
  • Hidden trump, and asking for it to be shown
  • Bid to take all the tricks
  • Bots with an adjustable memory to fill empty seats
  • House rules chosen by the host
  • Each side's points shown during the hand
  • Game points carried from hand to hand
  • No real money
Twenty Eight is in development and is not on Google Play yet.
