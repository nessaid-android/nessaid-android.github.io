Rummy

Rummy brings Indian 13-card rummy to a table of phones: everyone plays on their own phone, in the same room, with the cards kept private on each screen.

About Rummy

One phone hosts the table and the others join it with a short code. The host chooses how many seats, how many decks, which way play goes, and whether the first sequence must be made without a joker. Seats without a player can be filled with bots, each with a setting for how closely it follows the play.

The rules are the ones played at home: draw, discard, and declare when your hand holds two sequences, at least one of them pure. A cut joker makes its rank wild. A wrong declaration is simply refused, and you keep your turn. Scores run from hand to hand, with the usual penalty for dropping out early or late.

Your cards are sent only to your own phone. Nobody else's phone, including the host's, ever shows them.

Features

  • Indian 13-card rummy for 2 to 6 players
  • Each player on their own phone, in the same room
  • Bots to fill empty seats
  • Host settings: seats, 1 to 3 decks, direction of play, first sequence without a joker
  • Cut joker, pure and impure sequences, penalty scoring with drops
  • Wrong declarations are refused, not penalised
  • Sort and rearrange your hand
  • Running scores across hands
  • A table that survives the app being closed
  • No real money: scores are points only
Rummy is in development and is not on Google Play yet.
