2048 Puzzle

2048 Puzzle is the classic sliding-tile number game: slide the board, merge matching tiles, and build your way to 2048.

About 2048 Puzzle

Swipe to slide every tile on the 4×4 board. Two tiles with the same number merge into one worth both, and a new tile appears after every move. Reach the target to win, and keep playing afterwards if you like.

The target is yours to choose, from a quick game to 128 up to a long one to 16384. Three moves of undo get you out of a bad slide, and they are offered again when the board fills up. The game saves itself, so you can pick up where you left off.

Choose from twelve colour themes, each in dark, light or matching your phone, and share a winning board as a picture.

Features

  • Play to any target from 128 to 16384
  • Three moves of undo
  • Keep playing after you win
  • Score, moves and best score
  • Continue a saved game
  • 12 colour themes with dark, light or match device
  • Portrait and landscape
  • Swipe, or use a keyboard or D-pad
  • Share a win as a picture
  • No account needed
  • 9 languages
