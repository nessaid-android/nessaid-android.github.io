Teen Patti

Teen Patti is the classic three-card game for a group in one room, each player on their own phone, played for chips that exist only inside the game.

About Teen Patti

One phone hosts and the others join with a short code, over Wi-Fi, Bluetooth or Google Nearby. The host sets the number of players, the boot, the starting chips and the direction of play, and can sit out to deal. Seats without a player can be filled with bots.

Play blind or look at your cards and play seen, raise, pack, or ask for a show when two players remain. The pot and blind limits keep a hand from running on for ever. Hands rank from trail down through pure sequence, sequence, colour and pair to high card.

Your cards reach your phone only when you choose to look at them, and the table is saved after every move, so a closed app does not end the game.

Features

  • Classic three-card Teen Patti for 2 to 6 players
  • Each player on their own phone, over Wi-Fi, Bluetooth or Google Nearby
  • Bots to fill empty seats
  • Blind and seen play, raises, pack and show
  • Host settings: players, boot, starting chips, direction of play
  • Blind and pot limits
  • Cards sent to your phone only when you look
  • A table that survives the app being closed
  • No real money: chips cannot be bought, won or cashed out
Teen Patti is in development and is not on Google Play yet.
