Teen Patti Express

Teen Patti Express is the classic three-card game for friends on the move. Each player has their own phone, the phones connect directly with no internet, and the chips never were money in the first place.

About Teen Patti Express

A round of Teen Patti is quick, loud and made for a group, which is exactly what a journey needs. One phone hosts the table and the others join with a short code, over Bluetooth, Wi-Fi or Google Nearby. The host sets the boot, the starting chips, the number of players and the direction of play, and can sit out to deal. Bots fill any seat without a player.

Play blind or look at your cards and play seen, raise, pack, or ask for a show when two players remain. Pot and blind limits keep a hand from running to the next station. Hands rank from trail down through pure sequence, sequence, colour and pair to high card.

Your three cards reach your phone only when you choose to look at them, and no other phone ever shows them.

Made for the journey

  • No internet, no mobile data. The phones talk to each other directly, so a game carries on through tunnels, ghats and stretches with no signal at all.
  • Everyone on their own phone. Nobody passes a handset round the compartment, and nobody sees a hand that is not theirs.
  • Short of players? Bots take the empty seats, so two friends can still sit down to a full table.
  • A dropped link is not the end of the hand. A phone that loses the connection can join the same table again.
  • No sign-in and no real money. Install it, open it, play.

How playing together works, and tips for trains, buses and flights

Features

  • Classic three-card Teen Patti for 2 to 6 players
  • Connects over Bluetooth, Wi-Fi or Google Nearby, with no internet
  • Bots to fill empty seats
  • Blind and seen play, raises, pack and show
  • Host settings: players, boot, starting chips, direction of play
  • Blind and pot limits
  • Cards sent to your phone only when you look
  • No real money: chips cannot be bought, won or cashed out
Teen Patti Express is in development and is not on Google Play yet.
